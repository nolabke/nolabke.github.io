budget

A simple, private desktop app for tracking your money.

Java 🖥️ Windows · macOS · Linux 🌐 English · Polski · Deutsch 🏷️ v1.0.3

About

budget is a desktop application for personal finance management. It's built to be straightforward: track income and expenses, manage monthly budgets, and export financial reports to PDF — all without sending any data anywhere. Everything stays stored locally on your machine.

Features

  • Track income and expenses
  • Set and manage monthly budgets
  • Export financial reports to PDF
  • Available in English, Polish, and German
  • Runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux
  • 100% local — no data is transmitted externally

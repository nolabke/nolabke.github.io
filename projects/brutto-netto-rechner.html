brutto-netto-rechner

An interactive German gross-to-net salary calculator for 2026.

JavaScript ⚛️ React 🌐 German 💶 Germany · 2026 🖥️ Web application

About

brutto-netto-rechner is an interactive salary calculator designed to estimate net income from gross salary in Germany. Based on the 2026 tax and social security regulations, it provides a transparent breakdown of income tax, social contributions and take-home pay.

Users can customize their tax situation, compare all six German tax classes and explore how different salary levels affect their net income. The calculator also estimates employer social security contributions and total personnel costs.

Features

  • Calculate net salary from gross monthly or annual income
  • Support for all six German tax classes (Steuerklasse I–VI)
  • Calculation of income tax, solidarity surcharge and church tax
  • Social security contributions: pension, health, nursing care and unemployment insurance
  • Customizable health insurance additional contribution rate
  • Consideration of child-related factors
  • Support for Minijobs and Midijobs
  • Interactive comparison of net income across all tax classes
  • Dynamic chart showing the relationship between gross salary and net income
  • Detailed monthly and annual salary breakdown
  • Estimation of employer contributions and total employment costs
  • Based on German tax and social security parameters for 2026
  • Responsive, browser-based application

Technology

  • React — component-based user interface
  • JavaScript — salary calculation and application logic
  • HTML5 — application structure
  • CSS3 — responsive interface and visualizations
  • SVG — interactive salary chart

Disclaimer

The calculator provides estimated results for informational purposes only. Calculations are based on simplified assumptions and should not be considered an official payroll calculation or tax advice.
